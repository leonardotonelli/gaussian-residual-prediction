\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Predictive embeddings and collapse.} BYOL~\citep{grill2020byol} and I-JEPA~\citep{assran2023ijepa}, an image joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA), use an EMA target encoder with a stop-gradient to train predictive embeddings. Collapse can also be avoided without an EMA target, for example with a stop-gradient alone in SimSiam~\citep{chen2021simsiam} or with variance and covariance penalties in VICReg~\citep{bardes2022vicreg}. LeJEPA~\citep{balestriero2025lejepa} uses SIGReg, an isotropic-Gaussian regularizer based on the Epps--Pulley test~\citep{epps1983test}. LeWorldModel~\citep{maes2026lewm} applies SIGReg to an action-conditioned world model with one shared encoder and a deterministic predictor. Our Shared models adapt that recipe with negative cosine prediction, different architectures and fewer SIGReg directions; they are not reproductions of LeWorldModel (Appendices~\ref{app:sigreg} and~\ref{app:fidelity}).

\paragraph{Latent variables for uncertain futures.} Conditional variational models infer a latent from the paired output during training and use a conditional prior at inference~\citep{sohn2015cvae}. SV2P~\citep{babaeizadeh2018sv2p}, SVG~\citep{denton2018svg} and PlaNet~\citep{hafner2019planet} train stochastic predictors through pixel likelihoods. In embedding space, VJ-VCR~\citep{drozdov2024vjvcr} adds a discrete or sparse latent to a variance--covariance-regularized video JEPA with one shared encoder and no EMA target. Unlike \shvar{}'s residual, its latent is inferred from the observed future and has no learned prior to sample. AdaSSL~\citep{zhang2026adassl} adds a latent variable to the predictor of a self-supervised embedding objective. Its variational form, AdaSSL-V, uses a learned Gaussian prior and posterior, and its video model uses an EMA target; the \emavar{} is our adaptation of that recipe. \shvar{} places the same residual family in a shared, SIGReg-regularized model.

\paragraph{Probabilistic JEPA models.} VJEPA~\citep{huang2026vjepa} predicts a Gaussian distribution over target embeddings with an EMA target and a fixed prior, and is evaluated mainly for robustness to high-variance distractors, from Noisy-TV systems to image benchmarks and a control task. PhiNet v2~\citep{yamada2026phinetv2} learns video representations with a categorical stochastic latent, a variational objective with a conditional prior, and an EMA encoder. Var-JEPA~\citep{gogl2026varjepa} trains a coupled latent-variable model with an observation-space reconstruction likelihood, without EMA, on simulated and tabular data; in its simulation study it also ablates adding SIGReg to its objective and replacing its KL terms with SIGReg. \shvar{} uses a low-dimensional Gaussian residual, a learned conditional prior and SIGReg, with no EMA target and no reconstruction likelihood. We assess its forecasts with the Energy Score~\citep{gneiting2007scoring} after mapping predicted embeddings to physical coordinates.
